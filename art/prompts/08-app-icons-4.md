(extensions), ladybug (bug), nightcap smiley (emoji), arch + play cutout
(bridge), monitor + i (infocenter), signal tile (kdeconnect), outlined
checklist + pencil (menuedit), QR finders (qrca).

| Model | Settings |
|---|---|
| `recraftv4_1_utility_pro_vector` (SVG) | 1:1, 2048x2048, count 2, ×2 rounds |

Round 1 picked readable drafts; round 2 fixed review findings (bridge gets
a play-triangle video cue; kdeconnect a screen tile instead of a second
earbud set; menuedit a navy outline). `app-bluetooth` narrowed to
`blueman` only so System Settings keeps its own icon.

Adversarial review (all four angles): 8 pass with nits, 2 fail fixed as
above. Declined with reasons: micro-speck stripping (sub-pixel dirt,
invisible at every rendered size), 16px variants (icon-polish follow-up),
status-hue adjacency (carried by shape and context).
